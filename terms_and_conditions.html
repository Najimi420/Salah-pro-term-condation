1. Introduction

Welcome

Salah

Salah Pro has been created to help

the
five
daily prayers (Salah) with confidence

The

Asr,
Maghrib,

Isha,

Arabic
recitations,
transliterations,
English translations, and explanatory

In

Islamic

(Du'as),

references,

multilingual

application
